Above Ground Biomass


Above ground biomass products: NCEO Africa Biomass, IceSat-2 Boreal Biomass, JPL Global Biomass, GEDI Biomass, ESA CCI Biomass

Mapping aboveground biomass is a priority for several new space agency missions, including GEDI, ICESat-2, BIOMASS, ALOS-4 and NISAR. The biomass products provided by these missions can support the management of forests to support climate mitigation, supplementing ground-based data as typically collected for National Forest Inventories (NFIs).

Furthermore, space-based biomass data can provide critical data to ensure the quality and integrity of carbon credit programmes. The transparency offered by space-based data allows consumers to be confident when engaging with a carbon credit provider.

The CEOS Biomass Cal/Val Protocol aims to provide clear guidance on generating and using biomass products, to help mitigate variances between products which may lead to confusion and reduced confidence. Together with the Global Forest Observations Initiative (GFOI), CEOS Agencies are undertaking an activity to align space-based biomass products with user needs in a consistent manner. The goal is to understand and communicate discrepancies between products, and provide harmonised products based on specific needs of countries.

Below are several ongoing and demonstrator products which provide continental - global scale biomass maps.

CCI Biomass

The CCI BIOMASS project delivers spatially explicit estimates of AGB for four epochs and related standard deviations (SDs) as separate map products.

ICESat-2 Boreal Biomass 2020 Map

The ICESat-2 Boreal Biomass map for the year 2020 is a boreal-wide woody aboveground biomass density product.

NCEO Africa Aboveground Woody Biomass 2017

NCEO Africa Aboveground Woody Biomass (AGB) map for the year 2017 at 100 m spatial resolution.

JPL 2020 Global Biomass Dataset

This dataset (global above ground biomass density in Mg ha-1) shows the distribution of vegetation above ground live biomass (AGB) at 100 m (1-ha) spatial resolution.

GEDI Biomass Map

A 1x1 km spatial resolution mean aboveground biomass density (AGBD) map derived from GEDI lidar data.



CEOS ensures international coordination of civil space-based Earth observation programs and promotes exchange of data to optimize societal benefit and inform decision making for securing a prosperous and sustainable future for humankind.
