    Data Pathfinders by Topic

    Atmosphere

    The atmosphere is a gaseous envelope surrounding and protecting our planet from the intense radiation of the Sun.

    Biosphere

    The biosphere encompasses all life on Earth and extends from root systems to mountaintops and all depths of the ocean.

    Climate Indicators

    Climate Indicators hold key information for the most relevant domains of climate change: temperature and energy, atmospheric composition, ocean and water as well as the cryosphere.

    Cryosphere

    The cryosphere encompasses the frozen parts of Earth, including glaciers and ice sheets, sea ice, and any other frozen body of water.

    Human Dimensions

    The human dimensions discipline includes ways humans interact with the environment and how these interactions impact Earth’s systems.

    Land Surface

    The land surface discipline includes research into areas such as shrinking forests, warming land, and eroding soils.

    Ocean

    This vast, critical reservoir supports a diversity of life and helps regulate Earth’s climate.

    Solid Earth

    Processes occurring deep within Earth constantly are shaping landforms.

    Sun-Earth Interactions

    The Sun influences physical and chemical processes in Earth’s atmosphere.

    Terrestrial Hydrosphere

    The terrestrial hydrosphere includes water on the land surface and underground in the form of lakes, rivers, and groundwater along with total water storage.

SNWG Solutions

Satellite Needs Working Group (SNWG) solutions are developed by NASA's SNWG Implementation TEam (NSITE) in response to the needs of federal agencies identified through a biennial survey.

SNWG Solutions

Table of contents

SNWG Solutions Provided by NSITE

Image

Accessible 3D Topographic Mapping Software (A3TMS)

A3TMS, an SNWG solution, aims to produce a more user-friendly graphical interface for NASA’s Ames Stereo Pipeline (ASP) to improve usability and accessibility.

Read more about the A3TMS solution
Image

Air Quality Sensors and Forecasts

This SNWG solution expands current air quality networks, provides air quality forecast products for select locations, and produces PM2.5 forecasts for U.S. embassies and major cities worldwide.

Read more about the air quality sensors and forecasts solution
Image

ADMG-CASEI Solution

ADMG, an SNWG solution, improves access to airborne and field observations through NASA’s Catalog of Archived Suborbital Earth Science Investigations (CASEI) and advances data stewardship processes.

Read more about the ADMG-CASEI solution
Image

Animal Tracking (Internet of Animals)

The Internet of Animals study, an SNWG solution, strives to track animal movement from space to inform various conservation efforts and mitigate harmful impacts to ecosystems.

Read more about the Internet of Animals solution
Image

Data Curation for Discovery Solution

Data Curation for Discovery (DCD), an SNWG solution, assists other agencies in incorporating NASA Earth observation data into their workflows.

Read more about the DCD solution
Image

FIRMS-2G

This SNWG solution aims to enhance NASA's FIRMS to monitor and visualize smoke plumes, improve burned area estimates, and incorporate flexibility to ingest new sources of active fire data.

Read more about the FIRMS-2G solution
Image

GABAN

The Global Algal Blooms Assessment Network (GABAN), an SNWG solution, aims to provide an automated algal bloom hotspot product using data from multiple platforms and instruments.

Read more about the GABAN solution
Image

Global Cloud Composites from SatCORPS

This SNWG solution provides global high spatiotemporal resolution satellite observations that are needed to improve the representation of clouds and their effects in Earth system models.

Read more about the global cloud composites from SatCORPS solution
Image

Global NISAR Soil Moisture Product

SNWG enables a NISAR soil moisture product with 200-m resolution globally (approximately 400-m for the Sahara due to challenges of measuring soil moisture in dry sand).

Read more about the global NISAR soil moisture product solution
Image

GEOS Composition Reanalysis (GEOS-CR)

By assimilating atmospheric constituent data within GOES CoDAS, this SNWG solution produces a reanalysis product that provides a global, hourly, 3-dimensional characterization of atmospheric composition.

Read more about the GEOS composition reanalysis solution
Image

HLS Solutions

HLS, an SNWG solution, provides surface reflectance data on a common grid globally and is used to develop HLS-Vegetation Indices. Methods to reduce the latency to 6 hours are also being explored.

Read more about HLS solutions
Image

High Resolution Harmonized Land Surface Temperature (LST) within HLS

An SNWG solution that aims to produce a 30-meter, harmonized thermal product for use in applications such as OpenET, characterizing water use, and detecting crop stress.

Read more about the high resolution harmonized LST solution
Image

High-Resolution North America NISAR Data

This SNWG solution enabled the installation of an additional downlink station for NISAR, resulting in the highest resolution land imaging mode for North America and U.S. Territories.

Read more about the high-resolution North America NISAR data solution
Image

ICESat-2/ATLAS Quick Look Products

This SNWG solution reduces the latency of select ICESat-2 Advanced Topographic Laser Altimeter System (ATLAS) products from 45 days to 3 days to provide Quick Look versions to users.

Read more about the ICESat-2/ATLAS quick look products solution
Image

Merged PBL Temperature, Water Vapor, and Height Products

Using GNSS-RO and sounder data, this SNWG solution provides global atmospheric profiles of temperature and water vapor in the Planetary Boundary Layer (PBL), as well as PBL height.

Read more about the merged PBL temperature, water vapor, and height products solution
Image

Multisensor Worldwide Ocean Winds (MWOW) Products

This SNWG solution combines wind retrievals from multiple sensors to generate gridded wind speed data approximately every 6 hours, providing near-surface wind observations over the global ocean.

Read more about the MWOW products solution
Image

OPERA Near-Global DIST Product Suite

The OPERA Surface Disturbance (DIST) product suite, an SNWG solution, identifies when and where land surface disturbance (fires, landslides, etc.) has occurred at 30-m resolution every few days.

Read more about the OPERA near-global DIST product suite solution
Image

OPERA Near-Global DSWx Product Suite

The OPERA Dynamic Surface Water eXtent (DSWx) product suite, an SNWG solution, maps spatial extent of surface water on land at 30-m resolution every few days.

Read more about the OPERA near-global DSWx product suite solution
Image

OPERA North America DISP Product Suite

The OPERA Surface Displacement (DISP) product suite, an SNWG solution, identifies movement of the land surface from processes such as sinkholes, volcanic unrest, earthquakes, landslides, etc.

Read more about the OPERA North America DISP product suite solution
Image

OPERA North America VLM Product Suite

The OPERA Vertical Land Motion (VLM) product suite, an SNWG solution, aims to track the magnitude of vertical land surface motions using radar observations.

Read more about the OPERA North America VLM product suite solution
Image

Sea Surface Salinity with Sea Ice Mask

This SNWG solution adds a flag/variable to the Remote Sensing Systems Soil Moisture Active Passive (SMAP) - Sea Surface Salinity products to signify the fraction of sea ice pixels.

Read more about the sea surface salinity with sea ice mask solution
Image

Targeted Remote Sensing Training

This SNWG solution aims to deliver targeted training to agencies on the uses of satellite data for their decision-making processes.

Read more about the targeted remote sensing training solution
Image

TEMPO NRT and Enhanced Products

This SNWG solution delivers hourly, near real-time (NRT) air quality products from TEMPO and aims to provide seven additional science-quality products of trace gas measurements.

Read more about the TEMPO NRT and enhanced products solution
Image

Water Quality Products

Water quality products, an SNWG solution, use Sentinel-3 Ocean and Land Colour Instrument (OLCI) measurements to provide analysis-ready ocean color data products.

Read more about the water quality products solution

Commercial Satellite Data Acquisition (CSDA) Activities Informed by SNWG

Image

Access to DESIS Data

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, improves access to DESIS, a hyperspectral remote sensor aboard the ISS, data for the U.S. government.

Read more about the access to DESIS data solution
Image

Access to GHGSat Data

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, provides access to Greenhouse Gas Satellite (GHGSat) methane emission data at 48-hour latency through the U.S. government EULA.

Read more about the access to GHGSat data solution
Image

Access to High-Resolution DEMs

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, provides U.S. government access to 2-m DEM products: a near-Global Mosaic Earth DEM and DEM strips where new data was acquired for detailed change detection.

Read more about the access to high-resolution DEMs solution
Image

Broader Access to Planet Data

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, provides broader access to Planet data for U.S. federal civil agencies through the Expanded Planet EULA.

Read more about the broader access to Planet data solution
Image

Broader Access to Spire Data

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, improves access to GNSS-RO data through the U.S. government EULA.

Read more about the broader access to Spire data solution
Image

Discovery and Access of Commercial Data

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, provides access to commercial data through Earthdata Search so that both NASA and commercial data are discoverable in the same search tool.

Read more about the discovery and access of commercial data solution
Image

Unrestricted Products Derived from Planet Data per EULA

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, allows operational products derived from Planet data per a U.S. government EULA.

Read more about the unrestricted products derived from Planet data per EULA solution
Image

Unrestricted Use of Planet Data per EULA

This CSDA activity, informed by SNWG, provides U.S. government scientists and funded partners with access to operational Planet data per EULA.

Read more about the unrestricted use of Planet data per EULA solution
